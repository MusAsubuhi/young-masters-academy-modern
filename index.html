About Our School

A School Built Around Your Child

At Young Masters Academy, we believe that every child is a natural innovator — curious, creative, and full of potential. Our role is to give that potential the right environment to grow. Since 2005, we have served families in Likoni by combining the rigour of Kenya's Competency Based Curriculum with small class sizes, dedicated teachers, and a genuine culture of care. Here, academic excellence and personal development are not competing priorities. They go hand in hand.

Why Choose Us

We Are Here to Make a Confident Innovator Out of Your Child

Competent and Caring Teachers

Our teachers are CBC-trained professionals who are genuinely invested in every child they teach. The relationships built in our classrooms are what parents and alumni remember most.

A Personalised Learning Journey

Small class sizes mean we can tailor learning to each child's pace and strengths. No student is left behind, and no student is held back.

Holistic Development for a Changing World

From sports and music to chess, entrepreneurship, and languages — our co-curricular programme ensures students develop confidence and creativity alongside their academic skills.
