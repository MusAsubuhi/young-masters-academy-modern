About Our School

A School Built Around Your Child

At Young Masters Academy, we believe that every child is a natural innovator — curious, creative, and full of potential. Our role is to give that potential the right environment to grow. Since 2005, we have served families in Likoni through every change in Kenya's education system, from 8-4-4 to the Competency Based Curriculum and now Competency Based Education (CBE), and through all of it we have held to small class sizes, dedicated teachers, and a genuine culture of care. Here, academic excellence and personal development are not competing priorities. They go hand in hand. Explore how we teach and assess under CBE or see how to apply for admission.

A Note from the Directors

Welcome to Young Masters Academy

Karibu. This school began in 2005 with one conviction: that every child in Likoni deserves to be seen, taught well, and given room to grow. A family has carried that conviction ever since, and we carry it on behalf of those who first believed in it.

We have watched the national system move from 8-4-4 to the Competency Based Curriculum and now to Competency Based Education. Our task has stayed the same: know each child, build character alongside grades, and keep the school safe, honest, and well run.

Thank you for considering us with your child.

The Directors
Young Masters Academy, Likoni

Why Choose Us
