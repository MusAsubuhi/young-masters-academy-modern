Our CBC Curriculum: An Education That Goes Beyond the Classroom

Our Curriculum

What is the Competency Based Curriculum?

Kenya's Competency Based Curriculum (CBC) shifts the focus of education from rote memorisation to the development of skills, values, and competencies. Rather than asking ‘What does my child know?’, it asks ‘What can my child do?’ At Young Masters Academy, a Competency Based Curriculum school in Likoni, Mombasa, we have embraced this philosophy fully — in our teaching methods, our assessment approach, and the way our co-curricular activities complement classroom learning. Our teachers are trained and regularly updated in CBC delivery.

Academic Levels

From Daycare to Grade 9 — Every Stage Matters

Daycare & ECD
From 6 months

A Warm and Stimulating Start

Our Daycare and ECD programme provides a nurturing, structured environment for our youngest children. The focus is on safety, stimulation, and social development — building the trust, routines, and early language skills that lay the groundwork for all future learning.

Pre-Primary: PP1 & PP2
Ages 4 – 5

Where Learning and Play Meet

In PP1 and PP2, learners are introduced to early literacy in English and Kiswahili, basic numeracy, creative arts, and physical activity — within a CBC framework designed to make learning joyful, not pressured.

Lower Primary: Grades 1 – 3
Ages 6 – 9

Building Foundations That Last

Grades 1 to 3 cement literacy and numeracy while expanding each child's understanding of the world. Class teachers who know their students well deliver all CBC subject areas, and self-confidence is taught as deliberately as any academic skill.

Upper Primary: Grades 4 – 6
Ages 10 – 12

Growing Independence and Leadership

Upper Primary is a time of growing ownership. Students engage with more complex content, take on leadership roles, and develop through project-based learning and peer collaboration — skills central to the CBC philosophy.

Junior Secondary: Grades 7 – 9
Ages 13 – 15

Shaping Future Innovators

Subject specialist teachers deliver the full CBC Junior Secondary programme in Mombasa, including Pre-Technical Studies, Business Studies, and Agriculture alongside core subjects. Students are actively prepared for the next stage while developing critical thinking and personal clarity.

Beyond the Classroom

Co-Curricular Activities That Shape the Whole Child

Some of a child's most important growth happens outside of lessons. Every student at YMA is encouraged to explore and commit to at least one activity. Ready to see it for yourself? Apply now for our 2027 intake.

Sports & Athletics

Football, athletics, and physical education build teamwork, fitness, and resilience. Students participate in inter-school competitions throughout the year.

Music & Performing Arts

From singing to drama, our performing arts programme gives students a creative outlet and builds confidence in front of others.

Chess

More than a game — chess develops strategic thinking, patience, and concentration. Our club participates in regional competitions.

Entrepreneurship

Students learn to identify problems, propose solutions, and understand the value of hard work — skills for life as much as for business.

Languages

Beyond English and Kiswahili, we offer language enrichment that broadens communication skills and cultural awareness.
