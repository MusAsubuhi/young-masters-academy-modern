Your Child's Place at Young Masters Academy Starts Here

When to Apply

We Admit Students Twice a Year

Our main intake runs in January, at the start of the academic year. We also accept students at the start of the second term in May, subject to available spaces. Families are encouraged to apply early — our class sizes are intentionally small and places fill quickly.

How to Apply

Four Simple Steps to Joining Our School

1

Submit an Application

Complete the online application form through our admissions portal, or visit the school office in Likoni. Our team will acknowledge your application within two working days.

Apply Online →
2

Book a School Tour

We invite every prospective family to visit before making a decision. Meet our teachers, see our classrooms, and ask any questions. Tours run Monday to Friday between 9:00 am and 12:00 noon by appointment.

3

Assessment and Placement

For Grades 1 and above, we carry out a brief, informal placement assessment. This is not a test your child needs to prepare for — it simply helps us understand how to best support them from day one.

4

Enrolment Confirmation

Once a place is offered, enrolment is confirmed upon submission of required documents and payment of the registration fee. Your child's YMA journey begins.

What to Bring

Documents Required for Enrolment

  • ✓ Certified copy of the child's Birth Certificate
  • ✓ Two recent passport-size photographs of the child
  • ✓ Completed enrolment form (available at our office or online)
  • ✓ Previous school report or progress records (Grades 1 and above)
  • ✓ Transfer letter from previous school (for mid-year transfers)
  • ✓ Copy of parent or guardian's national ID

Not sure if you have everything? Come and see us — we are happy to help.

Ready to Take the Next Step?

Karibu — you are welcome here. Our admissions team is happy to answer any questions you have before you apply. Reach us by phone, WhatsApp, or simply come and visit us in Likoni.
